# Manual edition audit — 54 "done" sections vs the 2026 SLK manuals
Run 10 August 2026. Manuals: `Formation Solicitor/Manuals` (2026 editions).

## Headline
- **Chapter numbering and titles are unchanged** between the 2025 and 2026 editions. No misalignment cascade.
- **22 of 54** done sections carry a case or a statutory section number the 2026 chapter does not support.
- Only **6** of those are outright invented content. The rest are either an editorial change in the 2026 edition or unverifiable.
